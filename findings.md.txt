
# Startup Funding Analysis — Key Findings

## 1. Startup Funding Performance

**Highest total funding: DataNest — $10.05 million**

DataNest recorded the highest total funding among the startups in the simulated dataset, with $10.05 million across its recorded funding rounds.

**Most funding rounds: AgroLink — 3 rounds**

AgroLink had the highest number of recorded funding rounds, with three. The number of rounds measures funding activity, not the total amount raised.

## 2. Industry Analysis

**Highest total funding: ClimateTech — $12.8 million**

ClimateTech recorded the highest cumulative funding among the industries represented in the dataset.

**Highest average funding per round: E-commerce — $4.05 million**

E-commerce had the highest average funding amount per round in the results reviewed.

## 3. Geographic Analysis

**Highest total funding by country: South Africa — $18.15 million**

South Africa recorded the highest cumulative funding among the countries in the simulated dataset.

## 4. Investor Analysis

**Most investor participations: Savannah Ventures — 11**

Savannah Ventures appeared in 11 investor–funding-round records, the highest participation count in the results reviewed.

**Most lead-investor records: Savannah Ventures — 6**

Savannah Ventures was recorded as the lead investor six times, the highest count in the results reviewed.

## 5. Funding-Stage Analysis

**Highest total funding: Series A — $48 million**

Series A recorded the highest cumulative funding among the funding stages in the dataset.

**Highest average funding per round: Series A — approximately $5.33 million**

Series A also had the highest average funding amount per round in the results reviewed.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of SQL to explore data, combine related tables, calculate aggregate measures, rank results, and investigate funding patterns. It also provided practice with filtering, grouping, joins, and Common Table Expressions.

## Limitations

The dataset is simulated and small. These findings describe only the supplied practice data and should not be treated as actual startup investment statistics.
